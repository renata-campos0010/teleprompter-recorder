Welcome to the Teleprompter Recorder.

Click Edit text to paste your own script here, then Save text. Your script is saved in this browser only.

Turn on the camera, pick your microphone and check the green volume bar while you speak.

Press Record. After a 3, 2, 1 countdown the text starts scrolling and the recording begins.

When you finish, press Stop to watch your video. Then choose Retake or Save video.

Tip: put this window right below your camera and make the text column narrow, so it looks like you are talking to the lens.
